v1.2
Many dev files trimmed and/or removed, reducing the mod's filesize quite a bit.

Both forms' starting items now begin fully uncharged instead of fully charged.

Mustela's stats have been changed:
- Tears reduced to 3.84
- Mustela's Tears stat also grows or shrinks noticeably less extremely
- Base Damage increased from 0.85 to 1.50
- Damage Multiplier reduced from 2 to 1.28 (final starting stat of 1.92, up from 1.70)
Notes: This is intended to greatly reduce his overall DPS to compensate for his petrification effect

Tainted Mustela's stats have been changed:
- Base Damage changed from 1.57 to 1.7
- Damage Multiplier reduced from 2 to 1.5 (final starting stat of 2.55, down from 3.14)
Notes: This is intended to reduce his overall DPS slightly to compensate for his tears-turn-90-degrees effect making him rarely miss
- Range nerfed to intended stat value

Mustela's Medicine nerfed significantly:
- Now heals 3 hearts (down from full heal)
- Now only adds 0.5 Soul Hearts (down from 2.5)
- Now costs 2 hearts to take as a Devil Deal
- Now costs 32 cents (up from 15) if found as a shop item (such as from Chaos) or playing either form of Keeper
- Tears Down and Damage Up effects reduced slightly
- Item Weight for spawning reduced greatly
- Item weight decrement increased slightly (together this makes it show up significantly less often and fewer times in total)
Mustela's Medicine pity buff:
- Now adds 1 Heart Container
Notes: This is and always was a Quality 3 item and is also partly a joke about American healthcare, it's not meant to be the most balanced thing and granting HP and a tears down is the most straightforward way to make a common antidepressant into a TBOI item.

minor text fixes

removed herobrine

Coming soon:
- More mustela-lore-related items.

v1.3
Added a new item: Mustela's Tainted Sockets. (Quality: 2)
Almost all characters get these effects:
- 10% Tears Down
- 15% Damage Up
- 3 Range Up
- Adds the Brain Worm Trinket's effects to your tears, making them turn 90 degrees to avoid missing an enemy.
- Applies a costume of Tainted Mustela's eye sockets, blood tear stains, and blood-soaked body.
Tainted Mustela gets different effects:
- 8.5% Tears Down
- 25% Damage Up
- 1 Range Up
- 2 Luck Up
This item costs 3 hearts as a Devil Deal, and 13 cents if available for coins (keeper/tkeeper/chaos). It's found in the Devil, Greed Devil, Secret, Greed Secret, and ultra Secret Room Pools.

Added a new item: A Carnivore's Meal. (Quality: 1)
- HP Up (x1)
- Heals two red hearts
- this is just a generic "food HP up" that fits the theme of the mod
This item costs 8 cents if found in a way that costs coins (keepers and chaos), only found in the Boss pool.

Tainted Mustela now starts with 3 Broken Hearts (instead of two) as originally intended.

v1.4.0 (The Birthright Update)
Birthright effects for Mustela have been added:
[i]Eternal lift and ferret instinct[/i]
Mustela gains flight, a respectable Damage Up, and gains the skill of brutally destroying enemies from their weak points.

Birthright effects for Tainted Mustela have been added:
[i]A burning repopulation bomb[/i]
Tainted Mustela's tears become special ferret fetus tears that burn enemies on contact. His broken hearts are cleared and he also gets his eyes back...sort of.

Added a new item: Heart of the Mustelid.
- ACTIVE ITEM: 3 Charges
- Freezes all enemies in the room for ten seconds
- Once unfrozen, all enemies in the room remain slowed for 5 seconds
- 85% chance to spawn a Half Soul Heart
- 14.9% chance to spawn a Black Heart
- 0.1% chance to spawn an Eternal Heart
- Has a synergy with Car Battery
Heart of the Mustelid is found in the Secret, Greed Secret, Ultra Secret, and Treasure room pools, with a low weight.

Added a new item: Extant Broken Heart
- ACTIVE ITEM: 3 Charges
- Sets all enemies in the room on fire, damaging them for 4 seconds
- 33.4% chance to drop a Black Heart
Extant Broken Heart is found in the Secret, Greed Secret, Ultra Secret, Devil, and Greed Devil room pools, with a low weight.

Mustela's starting kit has been changed:
Mustela now starts with Heart of the Mustelid as his pocket active, with his uncharged Satanic Bible being moved to his regular Active slot

Tainted Mustela's starting kit has been changed:
Tainted Mustela now starts with Extant Broken Heart as his pocket active, with his uncharged Converter being moved to his regular Active slot

Tainted Mustela's stats have been changed:
Tainted Mustela cannot have his Luck go below -2 (down from 0)
Note that Tainted Mustela's starting Luck is still 0.

Items from this mod have been tweaked:
A Carnivore's Meal now counts as a Food item for the purposes of Binge Eater.

Mod support has been changed and updated:
Updated EID support to be cleaner, with simultaneously simpler and more accurate descriptions that don't instantly fill up the whole screen.
Added compatibility for the POG mod

Added over 40 new item costumes for both forms and streamlined costume compatibility for future expansion of costumes

Cleaned out more dev files to reduce the mod's filesize

Some bugs have been fixed:
Fixed an issue where Tainted Mustela's flight costume was invisible
Fixed a critical issue that prevented modded active items from working

minor text fixes

removed herobrine

Coming soon:
- More mustela-lore-related items.
- Custom controls image for the starting room of basement 1
- Even more costumes for vanilla items
